The Experience

What a visit feels like.

No rushing, no upselling, no noise. Just a calm room, a therapist who listens, and a treatment built around what your body is actually asking for.

The menu

Six ways to come back to yourself.

Not sure which is right? That's what the intake is for — tell us how you feel and we'll guide you.

01

Signature Relax Massage

Our most-requested treatment. A flowing full-body massage with pressure tailored to exactly where you carry tension — shoulders, lower back, wherever the week settled. You leave loose, light and genuinely calmer.

02

Deep Tissue & Recovery

Slower, firmer and more deliberate — for chronic knots, desk-bound posture and overworked muscles a gentle massage can't reach. Honest, focused work on the spots that have been nagging you for months.

03

Aromatherapy & Calm

A softer, sensory session led by essential oils chosen for how you want to feel. Designed to quiet a racing mind, ease anxiety and bring your whole system back down to rest.

04

Hot Stone Ritual

Smooth, warmed stones rest along the body to melt deep-set tension, ease stiffness and improve circulation. Deeply grounding — the kind of warmth you feel let go of in real time.

05

Restorative Body Treatment

A gentle exfoliating scrub and nourishing wrap to reset both skin and mind. You step out softer, lighter and noticeably more at ease in your own body.

06

Couples / Private Session

The same unhurried care, shared. A private space arranged for two — ideal when rest is something you'd rather not do alone. By arrangement.

How it works

Four unhurried steps.

1

A short intake

You start with a few quiet questions about your body, your stress and your sleep — so we understand what you actually need before you arrive.

2

Arrival into calm

Step into a low-light, phone-free private studio. A moment to settle, slow your breathing and leave the day at the door.

3

A treatment paced for you

Your therapist adjusts pressure, focus and pace to how you feel on the day — never a fixed routine, never watching the clock.

4

Time to come round

No being hurried out. You rest, rehydrate and return to yourself gently — carrying the calm with you, not losing it at the door.

Honestly

Is Mee-Spa right for you?

We're selective on purpose. This is for people who genuinely need rest — not a quick indulgence.

This is for you if…

  • ✓ You're carrying real tension — neck, back, shoulders, or a mind that won't switch off.
  • ✓ You're worn down by stress, poor sleep or a relentless schedule.
  • ✓ You want a therapist who listens and adjusts, not a fixed conveyor-belt routine.
  • ✓ You value privacy, quiet and being genuinely unhurried.

A gentle note

Mee-Spa is by appointment and intentionally selective. We'd rather give a few people our full attention than rush many through the door.
